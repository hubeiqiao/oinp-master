## What This Review Is For

You are reviewing copy and evidence strategy for `oinp.hubeiqiao.com`, a story-led public-awareness page by Joe Hu.

The page is not meant to be a formal policy memo, a legal argument, or a hiring funnel. It should use Joe's story as one concrete case to raise public awareness about whether Canada can recognize and keep early-stage builders who are already here and contributing.

Primary goals:

1. Share Joe's perspective.
2. Raise public awareness in Canada's tech, startup, university, media, and policy-adjacent communities.
3. Invite simple support and collect related stories.

Secondary goal:

- Let the right people contact Joe or build with Joe, but this must stay secondary.

## Important Constraints

- Keep the site story-led and human. Do not turn it into a government PDF or a policy brief.
- Do not make the hero copy too long. It is displayed over a video-led hero, so the opening must stay visually compact.
- Joe wants the page to sound personal, not like "Joe is a real example for policymakers."
- Do not make hiring or "Build with Joe" feel like the main purpose.
- Avoid brittle or over-accusatory claims such as "Canada makes world-class founders, then pushes them out."
- Use primary sources for policy links and factual claims.
- If recommending new links, please provide source title, URL, and exactly where it should appear.
