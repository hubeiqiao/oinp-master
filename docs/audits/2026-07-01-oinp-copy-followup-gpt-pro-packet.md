## Site Goal

The page uses Joe Hu's story as one concrete example to raise public awareness about a broader issue:

Canada and Ontario may be missing early-stage graduate builders who are already here, already building, and already contributing, but whose work does not yet fit one conventional full-time employer relationship.

The page should primarily:

1. Share Joe's perspective.
2. Raise public awareness in Canada's tech, startup, university, media, and policy-adjacent communities.
3. Invite support and related stories from people seeing the same pattern.

The page should secondarily let people learn more about Joe or contact him. Hiring/contact cannot dominate the page.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

- Keep the page story-led and human.
- Keep the page concise. This is a designed, video-led public-awareness site, not a policy memo.
- For any replacement copy, do not exceed the current copy's length unless you can justify why the extra words are necessary.
- Prefer plain language over OINP/IRCC jargon.
- Avoid "EOI" unless absolutely necessary. Most general readers do not know what it means.
- Use the full name "Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program" the first time it appears; after that, "OINP" is acceptable.
- Do not make the page sound like "Joe is the perfect example for policymakers." He is one example of a broader pattern.
- Do not make the page sound entitled, accusatory, or like special treatment for one person.
- Do not weaken the page into vague sympathy. The policy point still needs to be sharp.
- If you propose replacement copy, include current copy, proposed copy, and whether the proposed copy is shorter, equal, or longer.
