How we count

A number on an advocacy page is a claim. So here is exactly how this one is produced — and how you can check it. We would rather show a small honest number than a big unverifiable one.

The rules

  • One action per browser, de-duplicated. Refreshes and repeat visits never add up.
  • Bot-screened. A honeypot, a minimum time-on-page, per-network rate limits, and (when enabled) an invisible challenge. We do not claim perfect one-person-one-count.
  • Never seeded, padded, or purchased. The count starts at zero and only ever reflects real actions.
  • Hidden until it’s meaningful. The number stays private until it passes a threshold, so a tiny early count is never shown as social proof.
  • Append-only, hash-chained ledger. Every change is one entry chained to the last; the displayed total equals the number of active supporters and can be recomputed from the records.
  • Your own receipt. Supporting gives you a private receipt code — you can verify or remove your entry at any time.

Privacy

  • What we store. A random per-browser token so you’re counted once (no account, no tracking pixels). If you share a story, your name, email, and message.
  • Your email. Used only to follow up about this message — never shown publicly, never sold. Ask us to stop anytime.
  • Where it lives. Records are stored on Cloudflare and may be processed outside Canada.
  • Removal & correction. To remove or update anything, contact Joe at hi@hubeiqiao.com — your support and any story are deleted and the count adjusts.
